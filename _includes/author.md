I'm Edgar, a mechanical engineer by training, amateur street fighter at Fenway by Friday nights.

I'm a mechanical engineer at [REEKON Tools](https://reekon.tools/) in Boston building novel measuring devices and get them shipped. My role has evolved to more than just a mechanical engineer, mainly integration, jig making, testing, and also a roboticist doing some fun math, hardware integration, and design. Fun times!

I worked as a researcher at the Biomechanics Research Team [FTMD ITB](https://ftmd.itb.ac.id/en/) developing prosthetics, exoskeletons, and rehabilitation devices. I also used to make underwater robots at [Robomarine](https://www.robomarine.com/index.php) and soccer-playing robots at [Dagozilla](https://itb.ac.id/berita/dagozilla-robot-pesepakbola-ciptaan-mahasiswa-itb/56676) ITB!
