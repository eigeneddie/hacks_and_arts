Thinking out loud just for the LOLs

A note on what I care about on my spare time

I've just got back from Asia from a brutal work trip. For two months I had to endure things that fell apart, put them back together, and make sure they continuously run without me being there. I work in a startup. I love working there. I am now pretty much a senior engineer and have grown to be a highly versatile engineer. However, seemingly my two month speed run dealt a toll on me, and I needed a break. Not a vacation, a break.

I came back to Boston at the first week of November and decided to take a long break. As I was relaxing on a coffee shop around MIT campus, I stumbled on this podcast saying things like someone getting the job they didn't think to have. One thing that struck me was that thinking out loud in public shows that there are things you care about in your free time. There is some truth of that.
