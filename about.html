About

I'm Edgar, a mechanical engineer by training. I like taking things apart to see how they work, and building things with whatever's on hand.

eigeneddie::blog is my independent R&D notebook. I pick something I want to understand, whether that's a machine to tear down, a circuit to build or an idea to test, and write up what I find. That includes the parts that didn't work.

Why "eigen"?

In linear algebra, an eigenvector is a direction a system naturally moves in. In German, eigen means "own." This is my own work, going wherever my curiosity points.
